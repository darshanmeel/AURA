Does

  • Shows AI coding spend per day, session, project, helper agent and person.
  • Replays any past session: each prompt, reply, tool use and file touched.
  • Flags waste: big models on small jobs, failed tool runs, cut-off replies.
  • Keeps session data on your computer; blanks passwords and keys before storing.
  • Shows whether the numbers are current, on a pipeline-health page.

Doesn't

  • Doesn't read your provider's bill; costs are estimates from token counts.
  • Doesn't change or delete your session logs; it only reads them.
  • Doesn't cover other assistants yet; Gemini and Codex are planned.
  • Doesn't give a team-wide view; designed, not built (no central store, no sign-in).
  • Doesn't upload session data anywhere.

Limitations

  • Not real-time: new spend appears about 20–75 minutes after it happens.
  • Pages don't refresh themselves; reload to see new numbers (health page: every 10 s).
  • A new model shows no cost until someone adds it to the price list.
  • Opens on "Today"; a day with no sessions shows $0 — pick 7 days, 30 days or All time.
  • Needs room for two Docker containers: up to 5 GB and 512 MB memory.

Known issues, assumptions, and decisions needed

  • Total spend is the sum of priced replies; the splits and forecasts below can differ from it.

Known issues

  • App and agent cost splits, and the "All time" forecast, are wrong. Apps add up to about 6× real spend; the Apps page total about 2×; forecast about 6×; agent figures change with the date range. Use total and per-session spend for budgets. Confirmed
  • Privacy gaps: some secret formats (cloud keys, bearer tokens, passwords in web addresses) and session titles are stored unblanked; the dashboard is reachable from the local network without sign-in. Confirmed
  • Some screens mislead: the health page can say "All systems nominal" with stale summaries; saving a session verdict fails in the Docker setup (read in config, not run); the Errors date range has no effect. Confirmed
  • Costs of older sessions may be slightly high: cache writes without a breakdown are priced at the higher 1-hour rate (verified in the price formula; size not measured). Confirmed
  • An event being written exactly when Aura reads the file may be skipped for good. Not verified

Assumptions

  • Issue sizes come from a 2026-08-21 copy of real data; today's sizes may differ. Not verified
  • The 20–75 minute delay assumes a summary run of about 20 minutes, as noted in settings. Not verified

Decisions needed

  • Agent cost rule: per reply or per session; recommend per reply; until decided, agent figures differ between "All time" and date ranges.
  • Dashboard access: this computer only or whole local network; recommend this computer only; until decided, anyone on the same Wi-Fi can read all sessions.
