Does

  • Shows AI coding spend per day, session, project, helper agent and person.
  • Replays any past session: each prompt, reply, tool use and file touched.
  • Flags waste: big models on small jobs, failed tool runs, cut-off replies.
  • Keeps session data on your computer; blanks passwords and keys before storing.
  • Shows whether the numbers are current, on a pipeline-health page.

from README.md:20-24 · watcher/src/aura_watcher/redact.py:94 · docker-compose.yml:27

Doesn't

  • Doesn't read your provider's bill; costs are estimates from token counts.
  • Doesn't change or delete your session logs; it only reads them.
  • Doesn't cover other assistants yet; Gemini and Codex are planned.
  • Doesn't give a team-wide view; designed, not built (no central store, no sign-in).
  • Doesn't upload session data anywhere.

Limitations

  • Not real-time: new spend appears after about one save (30 s), the next 5-minute run and that run's time.
  • Pages don't refresh themselves; reload to see new numbers (health page: every 10 s).
  • A new model shows no cost until someone adds it to the price list.
  • Opens on "Today"; a day with no sessions shows $0 — pick 7 days, 30 days or All time.
  • Needs room for two Docker containers: up to 5 GB and 1.5 GB memory.

Known issues, assumptions, and decisions needed

  • Total spend matches the sum of every priced reply; the agent split below uses two rules.

Known issues

  • Agent figures mix two rules: spend is counted per reply, tool and commit counts by the session's main agent. Use total and per-session spend for budgets. Confirmed
  • The footer price list on every page is outdated (Opus shown at $15 in / $75 out; the price list has $5 / $25). Costs themselves come from the price list. Confirmed
  • A code comment says helper-agent turns have no prompt; they show the parent's prompt. Confirmed
  • Automated checks do not build the Docker images or run the Windows start script, and aura.toml is read by nothing. Confirmed
  • A full rebuild of the prompt summary needs the whole 4 GB memory pool. Confirmed
  • Prompts from sessions without a matching app may show no app; layer ages on the health page were not re-checked. Not verified

Assumptions

  • Run time of a warm 5-minute cycle is unknown; only a first full build (about 22 minutes) was measured, by the owner. Not verified
  • Cache writes without a 5-minute / 1-hour breakdown are priced at the 5-minute rate. Not verified

Decisions needed

  • Agent cost rule: per reply for spend, tools and commits, or per session; recommend per reply; until decided, agent figures mix two rules.
  • Footer prices: read from the price list or remove; recommend reading them; until decided, the footer shows old prices.
